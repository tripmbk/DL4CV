BIO

Luong Vuong Nguyen has been a Senior Lecturer and Researcher at the Faculty of Artificial Intelligence, FPT University, Da Nang, Viet Nam, since October 2022. He has also been an Adjunct Professor in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at the Catholic University of Korea, Bucheon, South Korea, since November 2022. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from Chung-Ang University, Seoul, Korea, in 2022, and an M.S. in the Department of Computer Engineering from The Da Nang University of Technology in Vietnam in April 2013. His research topics include knowledge engineering in data science by using data mining, machine learning, deep learning, ambient intelligence, and logical reasoning.
